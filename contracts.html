Six acquisition pathways

How agencies can order from us

Every route below ends with 20 59 Ventures as the party furnishing the hardware, the configuration, and the compliance documentation. What changes is the paper the order rides on.

Pathway A

Direct Award to 20 59 Ventures

The simplest route. We hold an active SAM.gov registration and can receive a direct award as the prime contractor for micro-purchases, FAR Part 13 simplified acquisitions, and orders placed with a Government Purchase Card. No vehicle and no partner in the middle.

Awardee: 20 59 Ventures Corp (CAGE 22DH9)
Vehicle: Direct award / FAR Part 13 / GPC
Best for: Micro-purchases < $10,000 and GPC orders
Pathway B

GSA Multiple Award Schedule

Order against GSA MAS contracts held by our Tier-1 distribution partner, and we furnish and deliver the product under that schedule with full manufacturer warranty and TAA-compliant sourcing. Agencies can create the delivery order directly in GSA Advantage against the schedule number below.

Awardee: TD SYNNEX Corporation
Vehicle: 47QTCA19D00MM • GS-35F-0563U
Our role: Authorized reseller under the schedule
Pathway C

2GIT Federal Blanket Purchase Agreements

TD SYNNEX's 2GIT program covers category IT products including hardware, software, cloud, and professional services on a federal BPA. Where an agency already holds a 2GIT ordering agreement, we can furnish against it under our distributor's award.

Awardee: TD SYNNEX Corporation
Vehicle: 47QTCA21A0026 • 47QTCA21A002E
Our role: Authorized reseller under the BPA
Pathway D

NASA SEWP VI

The federal vehicle for commercial off-the-shelf IT hardware, software, and services. Our distributor issues SEWP VI Letters of Authorization to authorized resellers, which is how we can quote and deliver against a SEWP task order for a federal end user.

Awardee: TD SYNNEX Corporation
Vehicle: NASA SEWP VI (reseller LOA required)
Our role: Authorized reseller holding an LOA
Pathway E

State, Local & Education Cooperatives

Cooperative purchasing vehicles let a state agency, county, municipality, school district, or higher education institution buy off an already-competed award without running its own solicitation. We hold our own OMNIA Partners NCPA reseller agreement through D&H, and our distribution partners are awardees on NASPO ValuePoint, TX DIR, PEPPM, Equalis, CMAS, and Sourcewell.

Awardee: OMNIA Partners (via D&H), TD SYNNEX Corporation & partner cooperatives
Vehicle: OMNIA NCPA • NASPO ValuePoint • TX DIR • PEPPM • Equalis • CMAS • Sourcewell
Our role: Authorized reseller / dealer
Pathway F

Prime Contractor Teaming Subcontract

We subcontract under primes that carry small-business subcontracting plans. Best fit for multi-SOW hardware deployments, enterprise refreshes, and agency-wide standardization projects where the prime holds the contract vehicle and we furnish the product and configuration.

Awardee: Prime contractor
Vehicle: Prime's contract vehicle (FAR 52.219-9 flow-down)
Our role: Subcontractor / teaming partner

Process

How ordering works

Three steps from first contact to delivered, warrantied, compliant hardware.

1

Send the requirement

Submit an RFQ on our portal with your vehicle, line items or OEM part numbers, delivery address, and deadline. Attach a SOW, BOM, or spreadsheet — we accept PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, and TXT up to 10 MB. Government Purchase Card holders can skip straight to a quote.

2

Receive a vehicle-ready quote

We price against live distributor inventory and return a line-item quote with CLIN structure, TAA and NDAA Section 889 certifications, lead times, and the contract number you should reference on the order. Micro-purchases under $10,000 are quoted within 2 to 4 business hours.

3

Order and receive

We deliver against your vehicle — direct award, GSA Advantage delivery order, or your cooperative's ordering portal. Every line ships with full manufacturer warranty through authorized North American distribution, and we stay on the hook for configuration, registration, and warranty coordination.

How we participate on each vehicle — stated plainly

We think a contracting officer deserves the exact answer, not a marketing line. Here is the difference between the two kinds of vehicle on this page:

  • Direct award to 20 59 Ventures Corp — we are the awardee. The purchase order comes to us directly, we carry the performance, and our CAGE code 22DH9 and UEI GVR2NTGR4HU3 appear on the order.
  • Distributor-held vehicles (GSA MAS, 2GIT, SEWP VI, NASPO, TX DIR, Equalis, PEPPM, CMAS, Sourcewell, and D&H's schedules) — the schedule awardee is our distribution partner. We are an authorized reseller on that award. The order is placed against their contract number, and we source, configure, and deliver the product under it.

Both routes give you 20 59 Ventures as the point of contact and the party responsible for the hardware you specified. We use the distributor's schedule because it carries TAA-compliant product, full manufacturer warranty, and the pricing already competed at federal scale — and because it lets a firm our size offer GSA, SEWP, and cooperative access without a multi-year application cycle.

If your agency requires the contractor of record to be 20 59 Ventures Corp, request a direct award or a teaming subcontract and we will quote it that way. We will never place a vehicle claim on your paperwork that we do not hold.

Common questions

Answers contracting officers ask first

Do you have your own GSA schedule?

Not at this time. We order against our distribution partner's GSA Multiple Award Schedule contracts — 47QTCA19D00MM and GS-35F-0563U — and we furnish and deliver as an authorized reseller. We do hold our own executed reseller agreement for the OMNIA Partners NCPA contract through D&H Distributing. If your agency needs 20 59 Ventures as the contractor of record, we will quote it as a direct award or a teaming subcontract instead.

Which vehicles cover our state or county?

That depends on the state. We hold an OMNIA Partners NCPA reseller agreement, and our distribution partners are awardees on NASPO ValuePoint, TX DIR, PEPPM, Equalis, CMAS, and Sourcewell. Eligibility is set by the cooperative and the state — NCPA, for example, requires the purchasing entity to hold membership. Give us your state and the product, and the contracting desk will confirm the exact vehicle and whether a membership is required.

Can you quote against a part number we already have?

Yes. Paste OEM part numbers and quantities into the RFQ line items field, or attach a BOM. We price against live distributor inventory, so a quoted part number and quantity is a real configuration rather than a placeholder.

What is the fastest way to buy?

A direct award with a GPC for anything under $10,000. Micro-purchases are quoted within 2 to 4 business hours and we accept Government Purchase Cards. Call the contracting desk at 1-888-919-2059 if you are working to a deadline.

Do you support DoD and intelligence work?

Yes — we are JCP certified and authorized to receive unclassified military critical technical data and export-controlled technical data, with a signed Export Control Compliance statement and a Data Custodian on file. We are also CMMC Level 2 certified for handling controlled unclassified information.

Can you work inside our procurement system?

We are set up to be purchased from inside agency systems rather than only by PDF quote — including punchout from Ariba, Coupa, SAP Ariba, and Jaggaer procurement environments. Tell us which system your team uses and we will confirm the ordering path with you.

Ready to order on your vehicle?

Submit your RFQ with the vehicle number, line items, and deadline — or call the contracting desk directly if you are working against a hard date.
