Process

How ordering works

Three steps from first contact to delivered, warrantied, compliant hardware.

1

Send the requirement

Submit an RFQ on our portal with your vehicle, line items or OEM part numbers, delivery address, and deadline. Attach a SOW, BOM, or spreadsheet — we accept PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, and TXT up to 10 MB. Government Purchase Card holders can skip straight to a quote.

2

Receive a vehicle-ready quote

We price against live inventory and return a line-item quote with CLIN structure, TAA and NDAA Section 889 certifications, lead times, and the contract number you should reference on the order. Micro-purchases under $10,000 are quoted within 2 to 4 business hours.

3

Order and receive

We deliver against your vehicle — direct award, GSA Advantage delivery order, or your cooperative’s ordering portal. Every line ships with full manufacturer warranty, and 20 59 Ventures stays on the hook for configuration, registration, and warranty coordination from quote to acceptance.

How this actually works — stated plainly

A contracting officer deserves the exact answer, not a marketing line. So here is the whole model:

  • Direct award to 20 59 Ventures Corp — we are the contractor of record. The purchase order comes to us directly, we carry the performance, and our CAGE code 22DH9 and UEI GVR2NTGR4HU3 appear on the order.
  • Schedule and cooperative vehicles (GSA MAS, 2GIT, NCPA, OMNIA, NASPO ValuePoint, TX DIR, PEPPM, Equalis, CMAS, Sourcewell, SEWP VI, and the state and education programs) — the vehicle is held by a schedule holder, and we hold the authorized reseller position on it. Your order is placed against that vehicle’s contract number so your procurement office can create the delivery order through the system it already uses.

Either way, you are dealing with 20 59 Ventures. We quote it. We source the exact configuration. We configure, ship, and register it. We carry the manufacturer warranty end to end. We answer for the shipment until it is in your hands and accepted — and if something is wrong, you call one person and it is fixed. The vehicle decides the paperwork. It does not decide who owns your problem.

Why route through a held schedule at all? Because it is what puts TAA-compliant product, full manufacturer warranty, and pricing already competed at federal scale behind a single small-business purchase order. It is also how a firm our size can offer GSA, SEWP, and cooperative access without a multi-year application cycle.

If your agency requires the contractor of record to be 20 59 Ventures Corp, request a direct award or a teaming subcontract and we will quote it that way. We will never place a vehicle claim on your paperwork that we do not hold.

Common questions

Answers contracting officers ask first

Do you have your own GSA schedule?

We do not hold a GSA schedule in our own name. We are an authorized reseller on the GSA Multiple Award Schedule contracts 47QTCA19D00MM and GS-35F-0563U, so you can create the delivery order in GSA Advantage and we furnish and deliver under it. We do hold our own executed reseller agreement for the OMNIA Partners NCPA contract. If your agency needs 20 59 Ventures as the contractor of record, we will quote it as a direct award or a teaming subcontract instead.

Which vehicles cover our state or county?

That depends on the state. We hold an OMNIA Partners NCPA reseller agreement, and we are set up on NASPO ValuePoint, TX DIR, PEPPM, Equalis, CMAS, and Sourcewell. Eligibility is set by the cooperative and the state — NCPA, for example, requires the purchasing entity to hold membership. Give us your state and the product, and the contracting desk will confirm the exact vehicle and whether a membership is required.

Can you quote against a part number we already have?

Yes. Paste OEM part numbers and quantities into the RFQ line items field, or attach a BOM. We price against live distributor inventory, so a quoted part number and quantity is a real configuration rather than a placeholder.

What is the fastest way to buy?

A direct award with a GPC for anything under $10,000. Micro-purchases are quoted within 2 to 4 business hours and we accept Government Purchase Cards. Call the contracting desk at 1-888-919-2059 if you are working to a deadline.

Do you support DoD and intelligence work?

Yes — we are JCP certified and authorized to receive unclassified military critical technical data and export-controlled technical data, with a signed Export Control Compliance statement and a Data Custodian on file. We are also CMMC Level 2 certified for handling controlled unclassified information.

Can you work inside our procurement system?

We are set up to be purchased from inside agency systems rather than only by PDF quote — including punchout from Ariba, Coupa, SAP Ariba, and Jaggaer procurement environments. Tell us which system your team uses and we will confirm the ordering path with you.
