### 1. YOUR ROOM & ALL-INCLUSIVE COMMUNITY LIVING

#### 1.1 100% All-Inclusive Living
We believe peace of mind starts with predictable, worry-free housing. Your program fee is **100% all-inclusive**. You will never receive a surprise bill for:
* **Electric, Gas, Water & Sewer Services**
* **Municipal Trash & Recycling Services**
* **High-Speed Wi-Fi Internet**
* **Professional Pest Control & Bi-Weekly Facility Restocking**
* **Full Bedroom Furniture & Linens** (Bed frame, mattress, bedding, dresser, nightstand, lamp)
* **Shared Living & Kitchen Amenities** (Smart TV, refrigerator, microwave, stove/oven, cookware)

#### 1.2 Your Personal Space & Bedroom Security
* **Privacy:** Your private bedroom is your personal sanctuary. Other residents are strictly prohibited from entering your bedroom without your explicit permission.
* **Access Devices & Smart Codes:** You will be provided with a unique digital keypad code or room key. For your personal safety and the security of your housemates, **never share your room key or front door access code with anyone**.
* **Care of Furnishings:** Treat your room and company-provided furniture with care. If an item needs repair or replacement, notify your Community Operations Coordinator (COC) right away.

#### 1.3 Proof of Address & Benefit Stabilization
Upon moving in, you will be issued an official, signed **Letter of Verification / Proof of Address** (`SOP-2059-FORM-002`). You can use this official letter to immediately update your address with:
* The Social Security Administration (SSA)
* The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)
* The Alabama Department of Public Safety (Driver's License / State ID)
* Food Assistance (SNAP) and Medicaid case offices

### 2. COMMUNITY HOUSE RULES & SHARED RESPECT

Because you share common spaces with other adults who value peace and rest, our community guidelines ensure everyone lives in comfort, dignity, and harmony.

#### 2.1 Quiet Hours: 10:00 PM – 7:00 AM Daily
* **Nightly Rest:** All residents are entitled to uninterrupted sleep. Between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM, noise levels must be kept to a strict minimum.
* **Media & Phones:** Use headphones or low volume for televisions, radios, musical instruments, and phone conversations during quiet hours.

#### 2.2 Visitor Policy: Daytime Only (No Overnight Guests)
* **Visiting Hours:** Approved daytime guests are welcome between **9:00 AM and 8:00 PM**.
* **Common Areas Only:** Guests are permitted in shared common areas (living room, front porch, outdoor patio) only when escorted by their host resident. Guests are never permitted in private bedrooms.
* **Strictly Zero Overnight Guests:** To protect resident safety and maintain program licensing, overnight guests are strictly prohibited. Having an unauthorized guest overnight is an automatic program violation.

#### 2.3 100% Smoke-Free & Vape-Free Indoor Policy
* **Zero Indoor Smoking:** Smoking of any kind (tobacco, cigars, pipes, herbal blends) and vaping or electronic cigarettes are **100% prohibited inside any part of the home**, including private bedrooms, bathrooms, hallways, and the garage.
* **Designated Outdoor Area:** Smoking and vaping are permitted outdoors only, at least **25 feet away** from all exterior doors and open windows. 
* **Butt Disposal:** All cigarette butts must be fully extinguished and placed in the designated fireproof outdoor ash container—never tossed in the yard or landscaping.

#### 2.4 Shared Kitchen, Food Storage & Bathroom Courtesy
* **Clean As You Go:** Sinks and counters must remain clear. Wash, dry, and put away your dishes, pots, and utensils immediately after preparing food.
* **Labeled Refrigerator Shelves:** Each resident has a dedicated, clearly labeled shelf/bin in the refrigerator and pantry. Respect your housemates: **never touch, use, or take another resident's food without explicit permission**.
* **Shared Bathrooms:** Leave the bathroom clean, wipe water from sinks and countertops after use, hang bath towels, and alert your COC if supplies are running low.

#### 2.5 Zero Tolerance Prohibitions
To ensure the absolute safety of every resident and staff member, the following carry **zero tolerance**:
* 🚫 **Weapons:** Strictly zero firearms, concealed weapons, hunting knives, or explosives on property.
* 🚫 **Physical Violence or Threats:** Verbal assault, threats, intimidation, harassment, or physical altercations.
* 🚫 **Illicit Substances:** Possession, use, manufacturing, or distribution of illegal drugs or non-prescribed controlled substances on company property.

### 3. MAINTENANCE REQUESTS & YOUR PRIVACY

#### 3.1 How to Report Maintenance Issues
When something needs repair, report it promptly to your Community Operations Coordinator (COC) via text, phone call, or email to `info@2059ventures.online`.

#### 3.2 Service Level Agreements (Response Times)
* **Priority 1 (Life-Safety & Emergency): Dispatch under 4 Hours**  
  *Total power loss, heating failure in freezing weather, AC failure in extreme heat, plumbing flooding, broken exterior door lock.*
* **Priority 2 (Urgent Repair): Dispatch under 24 Hours**  
  *Refrigerator failure, hot water heater outage, single toilet inoperable in house.*
* **Priority 3 (Routine Maintenance): Scheduled under 72 Hours**  
  *Slow sink drain, loose cabinet hardware, interior window blind repair, paint touch-up.*

#### 3.3 48-Hour Advance Notice of Entry
We honor and protect your personal privacy:
* **Private Bedroom Notice:** 20/59 Ventures staff or contractors will provide you with at least **48 hours written or text notice** prior to entering your private bedroom for non-emergency inspections, pest control, or scheduled repairs.
* **Emergency Entry Exception:** In genuine emergencies posing immediate danger to life, health, or property (such as an active water flood, fire, or medical crisis), authorized staff or first responders may enter immediately to resolve the hazard.
* **Common Area Access:** Common areas (living room, kitchen, hallways, utility rooms) are subject to bi-weekly routine walkthroughs by your COC to replenish supplies and ensure safety compliance.

### 4. HEALTH, SAFETY & CRISIS PROTOCOLS

#### 4.1 Quick Reference Emergency Contacts
* **Life-Safety Emergency (Fire, Medical, Overdose, Violence):** Call **`911`**
* **Veterans Crisis Line & Mental Health Support:** Call or Text **`988, Press 1`**
* **20/59 Ventures Program Director (Escalations):** Call **`205-534-8492`**
* **20/59 Ventures General Administration:** Call **`1-888-919-2059`**

#### 4.2 Overdose Prevention & Naloxone (Narcan)
Every 20/59 Ventures home is equipped with an emergency First Aid Kit located prominently in the kitchen or main hallway.
* **Narcan Nasal Spray:** The kit contains pre-packaged, unexpired **Naloxone (Narcan) nasal spray**, an easy-to-use medicine that rapidly reverses the effects of an opioid overdose.
* **What to Do if Someone is Unresponsive:**
  1. Call **`911`** immediately and state your exact property address.
  2. Peel open the blister pack, insert the nozzle into one nostril, and press the plunger firmly until it clicks.
  3. Roll the person onto their side into the **recovery position** to keep their airway clear.
  4. Stay with the individual until paramedics arrive.
* **Legal Protection:** Under the **Alabama Good Samaritan Act (Ala. Code § 22-2-19)**, any person who in good faith administers Naloxone or calls 911 during a suspected overdose is legally protected from civil liability.

#### 4.3 Fire Safety Protocol (R.A.C.E.)
* **R — Rescue:** Assist anyone in immediate danger if safe to do so.
* **A — Alarm:** Call **`911`** and loudly alert everyone in the house.
* **C — Confine:** Close doors behind you to slow the spread of smoke and flames.
* **E — Evacuate:** Exit immediately via the nearest door or window. Gather at the designated outdoor meeting spot at least **50 feet away** from the home (front street curb or designated tree).

#### 4.4 Severe Weather & Tornadoes
Alabama weather can change quickly. When a **Tornado Warning** is issued for your area:
* Immediately alert your housemates.
* Move to the innermost ground-floor windowless room (hallway, interior bathroom, or closet).
* Protect your head with blankets, pillows, or bicycle helmets. Stay away from exterior walls and windows until the official all-clear is given.

### 5. PROGRESSIVE ACCOUNTABILITY & GRIEVANCE RIGHTS

We believe in fairness, open communication, and second chances. Our progressive disciplinary system ensures clear expectations while holding all residents accountable to the peace and safety of the home.

#### 5.1 The 3-Strike Disciplinary Framework
Except for immediate severe safety violations, rule non-compliance is handled through our progressive 3-strike system:

* **Strike 1 — Written Warning Notice (`SOP-2059-FORM-004`)**  
  *Issued in person by staff. Outlines the violation and establishes a 30-day corrective action plan. A copy is sent to your sponsoring caseworker.*
* **Strike 2 — Final Written Warning**  
  *Formal written notice. Requires a mandatory joint conference between you, your COC/Program Director, and your caseworker to resolve ongoing challenges.*
* **Strike 3 — Notice of Programmatic Discharge (`SOP-2059-FORM-006`)**  
  *Issuance of a formal 7-day programmatic transition notice. 20/59 Ventures coordinates with your caseworker to arrange a safe handoff to alternative housing.*

#### 5.2 Clean Slate Policy (Expungement)
We recognize positive effort. If you maintain **sixty (60) consecutive days** of full compliance and clean community living following a violation, one active strike will be permanently expunged from your record.

#### 5.3 Severe Violations (Immediate Emergency Discharge)
Possession of firearms or deadly weapons, physical violence or threats against housemates/staff, or active distribution of illegal drugs bypass the progressive system and result in immediate programmatic discharge and police dispatch.

#### 5.4 Resident Grievance & Appeal Rights
Every resident has the right to be treated with dignity and fairness. If you believe a strike notice was issued in error, or if you have a concern regarding facility conditions or staff conduct:
1. **Informal Discussion:** First, discuss the matter openly with your Community Operations Coordinator (COC).
2. **Formal Written Grievance:** If unresolved, you or your caseworker may submit a written grievance by email to `info@2059ventures.online` or directly to Program Director Quincy Ruffin.
3. **Executive Review:** All formal grievances are reviewed directly by President Andrea Marcus and Program Director Quincy Ruffin within **five (5) business days**. You will receive a written response detailing the outcome.
