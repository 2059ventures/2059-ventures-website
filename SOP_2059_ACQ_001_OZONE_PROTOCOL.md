**20/59 Ventures Corp. | Property Acquisitions & Property Management SOP**

## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the mandatory protocol for conducting ozone deodorization and environmental sanitization treatments across residential properties owned, acquired, or managed by **20/59 Ventures Corp.** 

Ozone ($O_3$) treatment neutralizes organic and inorganic pollutants at the molecular level, restoring neutral indoor air quality and preventing odors from resurfacing after property turnover or renovation.

---

## 2. USAGE APPLICATIONS FOR PROPERTY ACQUISITION & TURNOVER

### 2.1. Standardized Tenant / Resident Turnover
* **Timing:** Executed in the turnover window between departing and incoming residents/participants.
* **Objective:** Restores neutral indoor air quality by eliminating deep-seated pet odors, lingering tobacco smoke, and cooked food oils embedded in wall paints, subfloors, and carpet pad backings.

### 2.2. Pre-Renovation Deodorization
* **Timing:** Executed immediately post trash-out/demolition, prior to painting or flooring installation.
* **Objective:** Sanitizes raw subfloors, exposed wall studs, and joists. Neutralizing organic compounds at this stage prevents old smells from bleeding back through fresh paint, primer, and new flooring materials.

### 2.3. HVAC Duct & Coil Treatment
* **Timing:** Executed concurrently with general space treatment or as part of routine HVAC turnover.
* **Objective:** Position the ozone generator unit near the main HVAC return grill with the thermostat fan set to **"ON"** (continuous mode). This circulates ozone gas throughout the entire ductwork network, plenum, and evaporator coils to destroy trapped dust, mildew spores, and microbial odors.

### 2.4. Quarterly Preventative Maintenance Treatment
* **Timing:** Mandated **once per quarter (every 90 days)** for all active residential properties owned, leased, or operated by 20/59 Ventures.
* **Objective:** Maintains baseline air quality, neutralizes accumulated bio-odors, prevents microbial build-up in HVAC systems, and mitigates allergen growth across long-term resident occupancies.
* **Resident Scheduling Protocol:** 
  * Provide residents/participants with a minimum **48-hour advance notice** of the scheduled quarterly treatment.
  * Schedule treatment windows during planned resident group outings, day programs, or off-site activities (minimum 4-hour window including run-time, breakdown, and ventilation).
  * Require temporary evacuation of all residents, staff, pets, and plants for the entire duration of the treatment and ventilation cycle.

## 3. MANDATORY STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

### 🚨 STEP 1: STRICT SAFETY PROTOCOL (MANDATORY)
> [!CAUTION]
> **UNOCCUPIED SPACES ONLY:** Ozone ($O_3$) is a powerful oxidizer and toxic to living tissue. 
> Under **NO** circumstances should humans, pets, or living plants remain inside the property during an active treatment cycle.

1. **Evacuate All Living Organisms:** Confirm zero human occupants, household pets, service animals, or indoor house plants remain in the facility.
2. **Door Warning Signs:** Post bold warning notices on all exterior entry doors prior to plugging in or activating the unit.
   * **Mandatory Text:** *"DANGER: OZONE TREATMENT IN PROGRESS – DO NOT ENTER"* (See Printable Sign in Section 4).

### 📋 STEP 2: PREPARATION & SEALING
1. **Seal Exterior Perimeter:** Close all exterior windows, doors, sliding glass doors, and garage access doors to prevent gas leakages.
2. **Open Interior Spaces:** Open all interior room doors, closet doors, cabinet doors, and built-in drawers to allow free flow and unhindered gas circulation throughout the structural footprint.
3. **Expose Problem Areas:** Pull back compromised carpet or open subfloors if heavy odor sources are present underneath.

### ⏱️ STEP 3: RUN TIME & TIMER SETTINGS
1. **Placement:** Place the ozone generator on a elevated, sturdy surface near the center of the structure or near the main HVAC return intake.
2. **HVAC Configuration:** Turn the main thermostat system fan setting to **"ON"** (not AUTO) to enable full ductwork distribution.
3. **Timer Selection:** Set the unit’s integrated timer based on total square footage and odor severity:
   * **Light Turnover / Mild Odor (1,500 – 2,500 sq. ft.):** 2 to 3 Hours.
   * **Moderate Odor (Smoke / Pets):** 4 to 6 Hours.
   * **Heavy Deodorization (Severe Smoke / Pre-Renovation Stud Sanitization):** 6 to 12 Hours (utilizing the built-in 12-hour mechanical timer).

### 🌬️ STEP 4: DISSIPATION PERIOD & SAFE RE-ENTRY
1. **Dwell / Breakdown Window:** Allow at least **30 to 60 minutes** AFTER the timer cycle completes before entering the property. Ozone ($O_3$) naturally decomposes back into pure diatomic oxygen ($O_2$) over time.
2. **Ventilation Flush Upon Re-entry:** 
   * Enter the home briefly while wearing an N95 or organic vapor respirator if needed.
   * Immediately open exterior doors and windows to establish cross-ventilation.
   * Keep exterior openings active for **10 to 15 minutes** to flush out any residual gaseous smells prior to resuming full crew work or resident move-in.

- [ ] Exterior windows, exterior doors, and exterior vents securely closed.
- [ ] Interior doors, closets, and cabinets opened wide.
- [ ] Warning signs posted on front, back, and garage entry doors.

- [ ] Ozone generator timer set (2 to 6+ hours) and unit energized.
