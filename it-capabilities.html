20 59 Ventures Corp is a verified small business specializing in commercial IT hardware fulfillment, systems integration, and technical workstation solutions for federal agencies, healthcare systems, and enterprise facilities. Backed by premier global distributor partnerships including TD SYNNEX and authorized manufacturer integrations with industry leaders like Ergotron and StarTech.com, we bridge the gap between Tier-1 technology and public-sector procurement. We deliver pre-configured workstation bundles, clinical cart integration, ergonomic mounting systems, technical furniture, and enterprise connectivity infrastructure across both simplified acquisitions and large-scale public-sector rollouts, with rapid Government Purchase Card (GPC) acceptance.
Authorized Distribution & OEM Partners: TD SYNNEX • StarTech.com • Ergotron • Microsoft • Samsung • Logitech

Core Competencies

Enterprise Workstation & Multi-Display Integration

Turnkey deployment kits combining multi-monitor mounting arrays, high-speed StarTech.com USB-C/Thunderbolt docking stations, KVM switches, and video connectivity solutions.

Ergonomic Technical Furniture & Facility Fixtures

Commercial-grade Ergotron sit-stand desk converters, height-adjustable tables, technical desking arrays, StarTech.com server enclosure racks, and cable raceways.

Clinical & Mobile Cart Hardware Refurbishment

Integration of Ergotron StyleView mobile medical carts, articulating clinical display mounts, power management accessories, and interface peripherals for VA medical centers and clinics.

IT Infrastructure, Power Distribution & Peripherals

Rack-mount Power Distribution Units (PDUs), StarTech.com connectivity cables & adapters, desktop power hubs, and TD SYNNEX authorized wholesale hardware fulfillment.

Key Differentiators

  • Tier-1 Distribution & Manufacturer Ecosystem: Backed by premier wholesale distribution through TD SYNNEX, granting direct access to multi-million dollar IT inventory, paired with authorized manufacturer integrations including Ergotron (ergonomic mounts & clinical carts), StarTech.com (connectivity & docking), Microsoft, Samsung, and Logitech.
  • End-to-End Workstation Packages: Single-source procurement for complete desk environments—combining Ergotron ergonomic furniture, StarTech.com docking/adapters, power distribution, and computing peripherals on one PO.
  • Micro-Purchase Agility: Dedicated support for Government Purchase Card (GPC/P-Card) transactions with same-day quote generation and accelerated shipping.
  • Dual Socioeconomic Set-Aside Eligibility: Certified VOSB (Veteran-Owned Small Business), EDWOSB, and WOSB (Women-Owned Small Business) helping agencies meet mandatory small business utilization goals.
  • Supply Chain Integrity & TAA Compliance: Sourced exclusively through authorized North American distribution via TD SYNNEX and certified OEM channels, ensuring full manufacturer warranties, TAA compliance where required, and zero counterfeit risk.
